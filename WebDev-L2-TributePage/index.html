Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a pivotal role in India's missile and nuclear programs and inspired millions through his vision for a developed India.

Achievements

  • Developed India's first indigenous satellite launch vehicle (SLV‑III).
  • Served as the Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister.
  • Authored inspirational books like *Wings of Fire* and *Ignited Minds*.
  • Received Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor.

Legacy

Dr. Kalam’s life continues to inspire young minds to dream big and work hard toward national development.
